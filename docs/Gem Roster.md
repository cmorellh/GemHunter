Rarity tiers are a game-design proposal, not market data. Hardness is the Mohs scale, which also drives which tool tier can cut the host rock and free the stone.

## Coverage

- **Phase 0 site (Ratnapura) yields #6, #13, #15:** common, uncommon and rare from one site, as the plan wants.
- **Minas Gerais is a strong second site** with four gems (#3, #10, #11, #12).
- **Every continent but Antarctica** has at least one node.
- **Hardness spans 5 to 10,** giving a natural tool-upgrade ladder.
