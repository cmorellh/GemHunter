## Not covered

- **Unreal Engine content.** Starter content, engine templates and anything else
  shipped with Unreal Engine stays under Epic's licence terms, not this one. See
  `NOTICE`.
- **Third-party assets.** Anything from Fab or another marketplace is generally
  **not redistributable**, which would stop contributors from building the game.
  Keep such assets out of the repository, or strictly optional. See `NOTICE`.
- **Reference photography.** Smithsonian Open Access items are per-item: the
  Geogallery listing mixes CC0 with "usage conditions apply". Check each object
  before using its image, and record the check.
